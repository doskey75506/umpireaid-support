UmpireAid Privacy Policy

UmpireAid does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Match data, player names, scores, and match history are stored locally on the user’s device. PDF reports are generated locally and shared only when the user explicitly uses the iOS share sheet.

UmpireAid does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, user accounts, or third-party data collection SDKs.
